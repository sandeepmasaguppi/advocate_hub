MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made on this _____ day of _______________, 2026, by and between:

PARTY A: __________________________________________________, having address at ______________________________________________________________________;
AND
PARTY B: __________________________________________________, having address at ______________________________________________________________________.

1. PURPOSE:
The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship / collaboration regarding ____________________________________________________ (the "Purpose").

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION:
"Confidential Information" refers to all proprietary data, financial figures, customer information, trade secrets, software algorithms, source code, designs, and business plans disclosed by either party.

3. OBLIGATIONS:
The Receiving Party agrees to maintain strict confidentiality, limit access to employees on need-to-know basis, and not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent.

4. EXCLUSIONS:
Confidential Information does not include information that is publicly known through no breach, already known prior to disclosure, or required to be disclosed by court order.

5. TERM:
This Agreement and the confidentiality obligations shall remain in effect for 3 (three) years from the date of execution.

6. INJUNCTION:
Both parties acknowledge that damages alone would be inadequate compensation for a breach, and the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief.

7. GOVERNING LAW:
Governed by Indian Law; Courts in ________________________, Karnataka shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

PARTY A: ___________________________           PARTY B: ___________________________
