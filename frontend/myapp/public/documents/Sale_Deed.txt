STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS & CHECKLIST:
- Governing Act: Transfer of Property Act 1882 (Sec 54), Registration Act 1908 (Sec 17), Karnataka Stamp Act 1957
- Stamp Duty: 5% Stamp Duty + 10% Cess + 2% Surcharge on Market/Guidance Value in Karnataka
- Registration: Compulsory Registration at Sub-Registrar Office under Section 17 Registration Act (2% Registration Fee)
- Notarization: Directly presented and executed before Sub-Registrar with two adult witnesses holding Aadhaar
- Competent Jurisdiction: Senior Civil Judge / District Court of the jurisdiction where immovable property is situated
- Limitation Period: 3 years for cancellation of deed or recovery of possession under Limitation Act
- Enclosures Required:
  * Registered Mother Deed / Previous Sale Deeds (30-year flow of title)
  * Khata Certificate and Khata Extract (e-Khata / A-Khata from BBMP/Gram Panchayat)
  * Latest Property Tax Paid Receipts and Encumbrance Certificate (EC - Form 15 for 30 years)
  * Sanctioned Building Plan & Occupancy Certificate (if apartment/commercial building)
  * PAN Card & Aadhaar Card of Vendor and Purchaser + TDS 194-IA 1% Challan
