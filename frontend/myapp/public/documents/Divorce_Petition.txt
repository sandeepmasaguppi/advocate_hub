STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS & CHECKLIST:
- Governing Act: Section 13-B Hindu Marriage Act 1955 / Section 28 Special Marriage Act 1954
- Stamp Duty: Court Fee Stamps as per Karnataka Court Fees and Suits Valuation Act (Nominal ₹20-₹100)
- Registration: Filed before the Principal Family Court Judge in District of jurisdiction
- Notarization: Supporting Affidavits must be sworn before Oath Commissioner / Notary Public
- Competent Jurisdiction: Family Court where marriage was solemnized, where parties last resided together, or where wife resides
- Limitation Period: Parties must have lived separately for a period of at least 1 (one) year before filing
- Enclosures Required:
  * Original Marriage Certificate / Wedding Card / Photos
  * Aadhaar Cards and Passport Size Photographs of both Spouses
  * Proof of Separation (Addresses reflecting separate living for 1+ year)
  * Settlement Agreement / Terms of Compromise (Alimony, Stridhan, Child Custody)
