STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS & CHECKLIST:
- Governing Act: Section 480 & 483 BNSS 2023 / Section 437, 439 CrPC 1973 (Regular Bail) or Section 482 BNSS / 438 CrPC (Anticipatory Bail)
- Stamp Duty: Court Fee Stamp (₹10 - ₹20) + Vakalatnama Welfare Stamp
- Registration: Filed before Judicial Magistrate / Principal District & Sessions Court
- Notarization: Affidavit of applicant's relative / surety to be attested before Oath Commissioner
- Competent Jurisdiction: Sessions Court of the district where crime is registered
- Limitation Period: May be filed anytime after arrest or upon apprehension of arrest
- Enclosures Required:
  * Copy of FIR and Complaint (FIR Form No. 1)
  * Remand Application / Charge Sheet (if filed)
  * Aadhaar Card and Local Solvency Proof of Surety
  * Medical Records (if bail sought on medical grounds)
