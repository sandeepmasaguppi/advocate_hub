STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS & CHECKLIST:
- Governing Act: Powers of Attorney Act 1882, Indian Stamp Act 1899, Registration Act 1908 (Sec 17(1A))
- Stamp Duty: ₹100 to ₹500 for family GPA; 5% Stamp Duty if GPA authorizes sale to non-family member in Karnataka
- Registration: Mandatory registration at Sub-Registrar Office if GPA gives power to sell immovable property
- Notarization: Attestation by Notary Public mandatory for general representation / administrative GPA
- Competent Jurisdiction: Jurisdiction of the area where property is situated or where acts are executed
- Limitation Period: Valid until revoked by Principal or until death of Principal/Attorney
- Enclosures Required:
  * Aadhaar Card and PAN Card of Principal & Power Agent
  * Recent Passport size photographs of Principal and Attorney
  * Property Title Deeds and Tax Receipts
  * Medical fitness certificate if Principal is a senior citizen

GENERAL POWER OF ATTORNEY (GPA)

TO ALL TO WHOM THESE PRESENTS SHALL COME, I,
__________________________________________________, S/o, D/o __________________________________________________, aged _____ years, residing at ______________________________________________________________________, bearing Aadhaar No. ________________________ (hereinafter called the "PRINCIPAL");

DO HEREBY APPOINT, NOMINATE, AND CONSTITUTE:
__________________________________________________, S/o, D/o __________________________________________________, aged _____ years, residing at ______________________________________________________________________, bearing Aadhaar No. ________________________ (hereinafter called the "ATTORNEY"), to be my true and lawful attorney to act for me and on my behalf in respect of my property described below:

PROPERTY DESCRIPTION:
Site/House No. ____________, situated at ____________________________________________________, Karnataka.

POWERS CONFERRED:
1. To manage, supervise, maintain, and administer the Schedule Property.
2. To represent the Principal before BBMP, BDA, BESCOM, BWSSB, Sub-Registrar, Revenue Departments, and Municipal Corporations.
3. To pay property taxes, electricity, water, and statutory dues and obtain receipts.
4. To sign agreements, applications, affidavits, petitions, and notices relating to administration.
5. To institute, prosecute, and defend civil or criminal legal proceedings in any court.

The Principal hereby ratifies and confirms all lawful acts done by the Attorney under this power. This GPA is revocable at the will of the Principal.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Principal has signed this GPA on this _____ day of _______________, 2026.

PRINCIPAL: ____________________________________
Signature & Thumb Impression

ATTORNEY ACCEPTANCE: __________________________
Signature of Attorney

WITNESSES:
1. __________________________________   2. __________________________________
