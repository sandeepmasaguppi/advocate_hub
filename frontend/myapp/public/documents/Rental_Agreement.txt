STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS & CHECKLIST:
- Governing Act: Transfer of Property Act 1882, Karnataka Rent Act 1999, Registration Act 1908 (Sec 17)
- Stamp Duty: ₹100 to ₹200 Non-Judicial e-Stamp Paper (for lease up to 11 months in Karnataka)
- Registration: Optional for agreements up to 11 months; Compulsory under Section 17 Registration Act if tenancy exceeds 11 months
- Notarization: Notarization before Notary Public recommended along with two adult witnesses
- Competent Jurisdiction: Jurisdiction of Civil Court / Rent Controller where the leased property is situated
- Limitation Period: 3 years from cause of action (for recovery of unpaid rent / security deposit)
- Enclosures Required:
  * Aadhaar Card / PAN Card of Landlord (Lessor)
  * Aadhaar Card / PAN Card of Tenant (Lessee)
  * Original Title Deed / Khata Extract of Property (Verification of Ownership)
  * Latest Electricity / Water Bill & Property Tax Paid Receipt
  * Two Passport Size Photographs of each party

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner in lawful physical possession of the residential/commercial premises bearing Door/Flat No. __________________, situated at ______________________________________________________________________, Karnataka (hereinafter referred to as the "SCHEDULE PREMISES").

AND WHEREAS the Lessee has approached the Lessor to take the Schedule Premises on rent for residential/commercial purpose and the Lessor has agreed to let out the same on the terms and conditions agreed hereunder:

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS UNDER:

1. TENANCY PERIOD:
The tenancy shall be for a fixed term of 11 (Eleven) calendar months commencing from ___/___/2026 and expiring on ___/___/2027. The agreement may be renewed mutually on fresh terms and conditions with written consent.

2. MONTHLY RENT:
The Lessee agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. ____________/- (Rupees _________________________________________ only), payable on or before the 5th day of every succeeding English calendar month via Bank Transfer / UPI / Cheque.
Account Details: Bank: __________________, A/c No: ______________________, IFSC: __________________.

3. INTEREST-FREE REFUNDABLE SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Lessee has paid a sum of Rs. ____________/- (Rupees _________________________________________ only) as interest-free refundable security deposit to the Lessor. The receipt whereof the Lessor hereby acknowledges. The said deposit shall be refunded to the Lessee upon handing over vacant physical possession of the premises, subject to deduction of unpaid rent, utility bills, or painting/damages charges.

4. UTILITY CHARGES & MAINTENANCE:
The Lessee shall promptly pay the monthly electricity consumption charges, water charges, and society maintenance charges as per actual meter readings and bills issued by BESCOM/BWSSB or Residents Welfare Association.

5. USE OF PREMISES:
The Lessee shall use the Schedule Premises strictly and exclusively for residential purposes and shall not conduct any commercial, hazardous, unlawful, or immoral activities therein, nor cause nuisance to neighbors.

6. REPAIRS & ALTERATIONS:
The Lessee shall keep the interior in good, tenantable condition. Minor day-to-day repairs (tap washers, fuse, bulbs) shall be borne by the Lessee. Major structural repairs shall be the responsibility of the Lessor. The Lessee shall not make any structural additions or alterations without prior written permission of the Lessor.

7. SUB-LETTING & ASSIGNMENT:
The Lessee shall not sub-let, assign, transfer, or part with the physical possession of the premises or any part thereof to any third party under any circumstances.

8. INSPECTION:
The Lessor or authorized representative shall have the right to inspect the premises at reasonable daytime hours upon giving 24 hours prior notice to the Lessee.

9. NOTICE PERIOD & TERMINATION:
Either party may terminate this agreement prior to the expiry of the 11-month term by giving 1 (One) month advance written notice or paying one month's rent in lieu thereof. If the Lessee defaults on rent payment for consecutive 2 months, the Lessor reserves the right to terminate the tenancy and seek eviction immediately.

10. PAINTING & VACATION:
At the time of vacating the premises, the Lessee shall restore the premises in the same clean and painted condition as received, or the Lessor shall deduct one month's rent towards painting charges from the security deposit.

11. JURISDICTION:
In the event of any dispute arising out of this agreement, the Civil Courts at ________________________, Karnataka shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
