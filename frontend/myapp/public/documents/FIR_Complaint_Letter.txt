STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS & CHECKLIST:
- Governing Act: Section 173 Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023 / Section 154 Code of Criminal Procedure 1973
- Stamp Duty: No court fee or stamp duty required (Statutory right to lodge complaint for cognizable offence)
- Registration: To be lodged directly at the jurisdictional Police Station having territorial jurisdiction
- Notarization: Not required; Self-signed complaint submitted in duplicate with acknowledgment seal
- Competent Jurisdiction: Jurisdictional Judicial Magistrate First Class (JMFC) having supervision over police station
- Limitation Period: Immediate reporting recommended; Delay must be explained reasonably in the complaint narrative
- Enclosures Required:
  * Government Photo Identity Proof of Complainant
  * Photographs / CCTV footage / Call audio records / Bank transaction statements (if applicable)
  * Medical Examination Report (MLC) in case of physical assault
