EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is entered into on this _____ day of _______________, 2026 by and between:

EMPLOYER:
M/s __________________________________________________ Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at ______________________________________________________________________, represented by its Authorized Signatory, __________________________________ (hereinafter "EMPLOYER");

AND

EMPLOYEE:
Mr./Ms. __________________________________________________,
S/o, D/o __________________________________________________,
Residing at ______________________________________________,
Aadhaar No.: __________________ | PAN: ____________________
(hereinafter "EMPLOYEE").

1. APPOINTMENT & DESIGNATION:
The Employer hereby employs the Employee in the position of ________________________________________, and the Employee accepts employment on the terms specified herein.

2. COMMENCEMENT & PROBATION:
The employment commences on ___/___/2026. The Employee shall be on probation for a period of 6 (six) months. Upon satisfactory completion, the Employer shall confirm the employment in writing.

3. COMPENSATION & CTC:
The Employee shall receive an annual Cost to Company (CTC) of Rs. ____________/- (Rupees _________________________________________ only), payable in monthly installments after statutory deductions (PF, ESI, TDS, Professional Tax).

4. CODE OF CONDUCT & CONFIDENTIALITY:
The Employee shall devote whole time and attention to the business of the Employer and shall strictly maintain absolute confidentiality of company intellectual property, trade secrets, client lists, and software code both during and after employment.

5. NON-COMPETE & NON-SOLICITATION:
The Employee covenants that during employment and for a period of 1 (one) year post termination, the Employee shall not solicit company clients or induce other employees to leave employment.

6. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT:
During probation, either party may terminate by 15 days written notice. After confirmation, either party may terminate by giving 30 (thirty) days written notice or payment of basic salary in lieu thereof. The Employer reserves the right to terminate employment immediately without notice for misconduct, fraud, or breach of confidentiality.

7. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION:
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and subject to the jurisdiction of competent courts in ________________________, Karnataka.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have signed this Agreement on the day and year first written above.
