STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS & CHECKLIST:
- Governing Act: Sections 138, 141, 142 Negotiable Instruments Act 1881, Section 223 BNSS 2023
- Stamp Duty: Court Fee on Complaint as per Karnataka Court Fees Act (usually based on cheque amount scale)
- Registration: Filed before the Judicial Magistrate First Class (JMFC) / Metropolitan Magistrate Court
- Notarization: Verification and Pre-Summoning Evidence Affidavit to be sworn before Notary Public
- Competent Jurisdiction: Court within whose local jurisdiction the bank branch where payee maintains account is situated (Bridgestone ruling)
- Limitation Period: Complaint must be filed within 30 days from the expiry of 15 days notice period
- Enclosures Required:
  * Original Cheque and Bank Return Memo showing 'Funds Insufficient' / 'Account Closed'
  * Office copy of Statutory Legal Notice sent to drawer
  * Postal Receipts and Online Postal Tracking Delivery Report / RPAD Acknowledgment
  * Bank Account Statement showing presentation and return of cheque
